The Lighthouse Keepers

Life, work and family on Kestrel Head, 1871 – 1989.

Dates1 September 2012 – 24 February 2013
LocationBond Store Gallery, Level 1
AdmissionIncluded with museum entry

For more than a century, the light on Kestrel Head was kept by hand. Three keepers and their families lived in the white cottages below the tower, and the oil had to be carried up 112 steps every evening.

The Lighthouse Keepers draws on log books, letters and photographs given to the museum by the families. It shows the routine of the watch, the school lessons by correspondence, the supply ketch that called once a fortnight and the storms of 1893 and 1921.

At the centre of the exhibition is the original first-order lens, lent by the Corrowa Coast Lights Trust and shown lit for the first time since the light was automated in 1989.
