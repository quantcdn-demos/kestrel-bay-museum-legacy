Collection Highlights

A few of the 14,000 objects, photographs and documents in the museum collection.

Showing 1 – 6 of 6« Previous | Next »

Pilot station signal lantern

KBMM 1987.044 | about 1880

Pilots on Kestrel Head used this lantern to signal to ships that waited off the bay for a pilot to guide them in.

Figurehead of the barque Marguerite

KBMM 1952.001 | 1861

The Marguerite struck Tallis Reef in June 1879. Three weeks later her figurehead washed ashore at Wirrega Point, almost undamaged.

Ship's wheel of the steamer Corrowa

KBMM 1961.009 | 1902

For more than fifty years the Corrowa carried passengers, mail and cargo between Port Corrowa, Kestrel Bay and the capital.

Ship's bell from the ketch Ada Bell

KBMM 2003.017 | 1926

The bell was cast for the Ada Bell when she was built at Mullet Creek in 1926. It rang the watches for more than fifty years of fishing on the coast.

Copper diving helmet

KBMM 1999.063 | about 1935

Before scuba equipment, divers wore heavy helmets and took their air through a hose from the boat above.

Ship in a bottle

KBMM 2008.205 | about 1910

Sailors made models like this one in their free hours at sea. The masts fold flat so that the ship goes in through the neck, and a thread raises them inside.
