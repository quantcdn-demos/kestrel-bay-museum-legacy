Membership

Members get free entry all year, invitations to exhibition openings and a discount in the shop and cafe.

Membership types

  • Individual $45 a year
  • Concession $35 a year
  • Family (2 adults and children under 16) $80 a year
  • Life membership $650

Member benefits

  • Free entry to the museum and all exhibitions
  • Ship's Log, our members' newsletter, four times a year
  • Invitations to openings and members' evenings
  • 10% off in the museum shop and the Jetty Cafe
  • Discounts on talks, tours and workshops

How to join

Join at the front desk, or call our membership officer on 02 5550 1842. You can also send an e-mail to members@kbmm.example.
