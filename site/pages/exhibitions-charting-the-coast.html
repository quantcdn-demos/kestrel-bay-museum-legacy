Charting the Coast

Survey ships, sea charts and the instruments that made them.

Dates15 March – 30 November 2012
LocationMap Room, Level 1
AdmissionIncluded with museum entry

Before the coast could be settled, it had to be charted. Charting the Coast follows the survey ship Petrel along the Corrowa Coast in 1863 and 1864, as its officers sounded the bay and named Wirrega Point and the Dunmore Shoals.

The exhibition shows original charts from the museum's map collection, with the sextants, chronometers and lead lines used to make them.

Children can try a simple survey of their own on the Map Room floor.

Gallery

  • A weathered copper and brass signal lantern with a green glass globe
  • A teak and brass ship's steering wheel
  • The museum foyer, with timber floors and tall windows
