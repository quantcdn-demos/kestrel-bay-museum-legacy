Welcome aboard

Since 1952 the Kestrel Bay Maritime Museum has collected the stories of the Corrowa Coast: its ships and wrecks, its lighthouse and its boatbuilders. Our galleries fill the sandstone Old Customs House and the restored bond store on the foreshore.

This spring, step inside the lamp room in The Lighthouse Keepers, meet the Marguerite figurehead after her restoration, and sing along at our Sea Shanty Night.
