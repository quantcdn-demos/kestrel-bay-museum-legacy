Sea Shanty Night

Songs of the sea from the Kestrel Bay Shanty Crew, with supper among the boats.

DateFriday 19 October 2012
Time7.00pm – 9.30pm
PriceAdults $25, members $20, children under 16 free
VenueHarbour Gallery

Join the Kestrel Bay Shanty Crew for an evening of work songs, ballads and sing-alongs in the Harbour Gallery. The crew have sung at the museum every spring since 1998.

A light supper of local fish and chips is served from 6.30pm. The bar is open all evening.

Bookings are essential. Seats are not reserved.
