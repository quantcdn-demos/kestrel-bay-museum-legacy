Figureheads and Carvers

Seven figureheads, restored and shown together for the first time.

Dates6 October 2012 – 28 April 2013
LocationLong Room, Level 1
AdmissionIncluded with museum entry

A ship's figurehead was its guardian and its signature. This exhibition shows seven figureheads from ships lost or broken up on the Corrowa Coast, among them the Marguerite figurehead, which washed ashore at Wirrega Point three weeks after the wreck.

Conservators from the museum spent two years removing old paint and repairing the timber. The exhibition explains their work, with tools, paint samples and photographs.

Local carvers show their craft in the Long Room on the first Sunday of each month.
