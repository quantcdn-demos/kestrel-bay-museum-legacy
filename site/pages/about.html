About the museum

The Kestrel Bay Maritime Museum collects, keeps and shares the maritime history of Kestrel Bay and the Corrowa Coast.

Our story

The museum began in 1952, when a group of townspeople put the Marguerite figurehead on show in the old Customs House. Today the collection holds more than 14,000 objects, photographs and documents.

The Customs House was built in 1868 from local sandstone. The museum restored the building and the bond store next door between 2004 and 2009.

Our people

The museum is a not-for-profit organisation run by a board of eight directors. A staff of eleven works with more than 140 volunteers, who guide tours, restore boats and care for the collection.

Support the museum

The museum relies on admissions, membership and gifts. Donations of $2 or more are tax deductible. To discuss a gift or a bequest, contact the Director on 02 5550 1840.
