Collection highlight

Copper diving helmet

Accession numberKBMM 1999.063
Dateabout 1935
MaterialsCopper, brass, glass
ProvenanceUsed by salvage diver Arthur Penhale on the Southern Reef wrecks. Given by his grandson, 1999.

The story

Before scuba equipment, divers wore heavy helmets and took their air through a hose from the boat above.

Arthur Penhale wore this helmet for salvage work on Tallis Reef in the 1930s and 1940s. His logs are in the museum archive.
