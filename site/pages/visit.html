Visit us

The museum is in the Old Customs House on the Kestrel Bay foreshore, a short walk from the town centre.

Opening hours

Open daily, 9.30am – 4.30pm. Last entry at 4.00pm.

Closed Christmas Day and Good Friday. Open on public holidays.

Admission

Adults $14 | Concession $10 | Children 5 – 15 $7 | Children under 5 free

Family (2 adults and up to 3 children) $35

Members free. Admission includes entry to all exhibitions and the Slipway Shed.

Getting here

By car: from the Corrowa Coast Highway, take the Kestrel Bay exit and follow the signs to the foreshore. Free parking is on Foreshore Parade.

By bus: Route 412 from Port Corrowa stops at the Foreshore Parade stop, 100 metres from the museum.

By boat: visitor moorings are at the museum jetty. Please call 02 5550 1840 before you arrive.

Accessibility

All galleries are on level ground or reached by lift. Wheelchairs are free to borrow at the front desk. Assistance dogs are welcome.

Cafe and shop

The Jetty Cafe is open 9.30am – 4.00pm. The museum shop sells books, maps, prints and gifts.
